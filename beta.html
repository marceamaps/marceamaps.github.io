Strava Mobile Optimization

Redesigning the mobile record tracking experience to reduce friction and improve user engagement during workout sessions.

YEAR

2024

CLIENT

Strava (concept project)

SCOPE

Mobile UX redesign, user research, prototyping

Key Problems

Users were experiencing friction in the mobile recording flow, leading to incomplete workout sessions and reduced app engagement.

The current interface required too many taps to start recording, and mid-workout controls were difficult to access while exercising.

Design Approach

I focused on streamlining the recording flow and making key actions accessible with one hand during workouts.

The solution prioritized larger touch targets, simplified navigation, and contextual information display.
